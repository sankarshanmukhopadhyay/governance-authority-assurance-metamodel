# Authority and Delegation Model
Authority is resolved from an attributable source, constrained by scope and lifecycle, and bound to accountable parties. Delegation must attenuate parent authority and may not survive suspension, revocation or expiry of its parent. Technical capability is not treated as proof of authority.
