# Obligation Lifecycle

{% include gaam-meta.html %}

> This is an informative candidate concept for evaluation. It does not modify GAAM v0.9.0 requirements, schemas, profiles, vocabularies, or conformance semantics.

Obligations can arise before, during, or after execution, revocation, harm, appeal, or termination. Candidate states include created, assigned, accepted, due, fulfilled, breached, waived, superseded, remedied, and closed.

Decision and remedy records should identify the responsible party, deadline, evidence of fulfilment, downstream dependencies, breach consequences, and escalation route.
