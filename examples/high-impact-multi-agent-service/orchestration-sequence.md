# Orchestration Sequence
1. Resolve the orchestrator and specialist-agent authorities.
2. Verify component state and evidence freshness.
3. Enforce advisory versus execution separation.
4. Collect attributable component outputs.
5. Detect conflicts, uncertainty and mandatory review triggers.
6. Obtain human approval where required.
7. Enforce the composite outcome.
8. Preserve evidence lineage and review linkage.
