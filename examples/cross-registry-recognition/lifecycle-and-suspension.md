# Recognition Lifecycle and Suspension
Recognition moves through proposed, active, restricted, suspended, revoked, expired and superseded states. Suspension blocks new reliance while preserving attributable historical evidence. Cached recognition state has a bounded lifetime and is invalidated by authoritative withdrawal notice.
