# Notice, Review, and Remedy
A materially affected party receives intelligible notice of the decision, accountable authority, principal reasons, evidence-access route, review route and available interim protection. Review is functionally independent of the original decision. Remedy evidence confirms that reversal or correction propagated to downstream systems.
