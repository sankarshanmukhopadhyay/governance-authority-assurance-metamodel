# Runtime Decision Sequence
1. Resolve the current authority and applicable policy.
2. Check scope, time, lifecycle state and delegation constraints.
3. Evaluate evidence freshness and assurance.
4. Decide permit, deny, restrict, request evidence or route for review.
5. Enforce the decision at the effect boundary.
6. Produce a proportionate receipt.
7. Preserve notice, review and remedy linkage.
